Everything here builds on one object. A store, created once from your drizzle instance, holds the threads and messages; the rest of the API is what that store can do and the route handlers that drive it.

New to the package? [Getting started](/en/docs/getting-started) is a shorter path in.

## The route

[`chatHandler`](/en/docs/api/chat-handler) is the whole chat route: it loads the thread, stores the incoming message, streams the answer, and stores the reply. Every option, what it does in order, and how to secure a thread.

[`resumableChat`](/en/docs/api/resumable-chat) adds the POST, GET and DELETE trio, so a reload mid-answer picks the stream back up. Covers the Redis-backed context you need beyond a single instance.

## Storing and reading

[The store](/en/docs/api/store) covers `createThreadStore` and every thread and message method, keyset pagination, `orderPath`, and how many round trips each operation costs.

[Branching](/en/docs/api/branching) is regenerate, edit-and-fork and sibling navigation - the tree behind them, and why nothing is ever overwritten.

## Converting

[`convertToUIMessages`](/en/docs/api/convert) turns `ModelMessage[]` back into `UIMessage[]`, the direction the AI SDK still does not ship.

## Your database

[Schema](/en/docs/api/schema) lists both tables, every column, and why the timestamps are millisecond precision.

[SQLite](/en/docs/api/sqlite) is the same contract over SQLite, plus the three constraints that are not optional.

## Migrating

Moving between AI SDK majors, or arriving from elsewhere? [Migrating](/en/docs/migrating) explains `sdk_version` and the `migrate` CLI, and [Importing](/en/docs/importing) brings over the Vercel `ai-chatbot` template's tables.
